Peak 3.3 is a system-integration release on top of 3.2. Private by default. No accounts.

Find your sessions from Search
• Sessions, spots, and gear appear in Spotlight on iOS 18
• Open a session, break, or board from Siri or Search
• Ask “When did I last surf?” and jump straight to that session

iPad
• A sidebar on iPad with Search, plus Spots and Buddies in the Library
• History, Quiver, and Spots use a split view on a wide screen

Apple Health, one tap from Log
• When Health sync is on, the Log tab offers the latest unlogged Apple Watch surf
• Optional local notification when a Watch surf is ready to log (off by default)
• Session detail can show a map of that workout’s GPS route. Coordinates are never saved.

Widgets that go where you expect
• Last Session opens that session, not a blank log
• Start Session on the medium and extra-large Last Session widgets
• Extra-large Last Session, and a configurable spot, from the snapshot Peak already publishes

Wave stats stay estimates, and your edits still win. Nothing leaves the device.
