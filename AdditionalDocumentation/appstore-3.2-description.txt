Peak is a fast, private surf-session logbook built for surfers.

Log a session in seconds:
• Date + spot (location pin optional)
• Gear used (boards, wetsuits, fins, leashes, and more)
• Who you surfed with
• Star rating + optional notes
• Optional photos and videos
• Or start a timer when you paddle out and end it when you come in

Your surf history at a glance:
• Timeline with search and filters (spot, gear, buddy, rating, dates)
• Stats — time in the water, week streaks, consistency heatmap, monthly charts, top spots and gear
• Board Report — which board actually earns its keep, broken down by wave height and swell period
• Monthly goals in sessions or hours, with a progress ring
• Wave records — most waves in a session, fastest wave, longest ride
• On This Day and Year in Review, so the logbook gives something back
• Spots map for the breaks you've pinned

Conditions, recorded with the session:
• One-tap auto-fill for wind, waves, swell, water temperature, and tide
• Best Window Today scores the next 24 hours at your most-logged breaks against the sessions you've rated there — and tells you when your logbook doesn't yet carry enough signal to say
• Readings in your local units (ft/mph/°F or m/km/h/°C)

Apple Health, entirely optional:
• Save your surf sessions to Health as surfing workouts
• Import surf workouts recorded elsewhere, including on Apple Watch
• From an imported workout's GPS route, Peak can estimate wave count, top speed, longest ride, and paddle distance — worked out on your iPhone, with nothing sent anywhere
• Every one of those figures is labelled an estimate and every one is editable. Your correction always wins, and a later import will never overwrite it.

Widgets and shortcuts:
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets for your week streak and your last session
• A Control Center button to start a session without opening the app
• Siri and Shortcuts: log a session, start or end one, or ask when you last surfed
• A Live Activity that counts the session on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island

Designed for iOS:
• Full Light and Dark Mode
• Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, and Reduce Motion support
• A clean, surf-inspired design that stays out of your way
• On iPhone models that support Apple Intelligence, your monthly recap is phrased on-device. Every number in it is computed by Peak from your own logbook — the model only supplies the wording, and nothing leaves your phone. On every other iPhone the same facts render as plain stats.

Private by default:
• No accounts
• No social features
• No analytics, no tracking, no ads
• Your data stays on your device
• Export to JSON or CSV, or take a full backup including photos and videos, whenever you want

Go surf. Log it. Improve.
