Peak 3.2 is a big one: everything built since 2.6, released together.

Widgets, Siri, and the Lock Screen
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets for your week streak and your last session
• A Live Activity that counts your session on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island
• A Control Center button that starts a session without opening the app
• Siri and Shortcuts: log a session, start or end one, or ask when you last surfed

Wave stats from Apple Health
• Import a surf workout and Peak estimates wave count, top speed, longest ride, and paddle distance from its GPS route — worked out on your iPhone, with nothing sent anywhere
• Every figure is labelled an estimate and every one is editable. Your correction is never overwritten by a later import.
• New wave records on Stats: most waves in a session, fastest wave, longest ride

Best Window Today
• Scores the next 24 hours at your most-logged breaks against the sessions you have actually rated there, and cites the past session it resembles
• When your logbook does not yet carry enough signal, it says so instead of guessing
• Tide now joins the conditions Peak records, with a rising, high, falling, or low trend

Knowing your quiver
• Board Report: how each board rates across wave heights and swell periods, with a floor of three rated sessions before Peak will call it a pattern
• Monthly goals in sessions or hours, with a progress ring
• On This Day resurfaces a session from a previous year
• Year in Review turns your season into one shareable card

Recaps
• A monthly recap on Stats, and a written recap in Year in Review
• On iPhone models that support Apple Intelligence the phrasing is generated on-device; every number is computed by Peak itself. On every other iPhone the same facts render as plain stats.

Also in this release
• A first-run welcome and in-app tips
• A broad accessibility pass: larger touch targets, better VoiceOver labels, Reduce Transparency support, and Dynamic Type fixes throughout
• Fixes to conditions maths, backup and restore integrity, widget freshness, and Siri's view of your logbook

Private by default. No accounts. Your data stays on your device.
