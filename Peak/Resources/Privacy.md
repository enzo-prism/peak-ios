# Peak Privacy Policy

Peak is private by default. Your surf sessions live on your device. We do not require accounts, and we do not use analytics or tracking SDKs.

## On-device data
Peak stores sessions, spots, gear, buddies, photos, videos, and settings **locally** using on-device storage (SwiftData and app file storage). That data stays on your device unless **you** export a backup, share a session card, or choose optional network features below.

## Optional features that use network or system APIs

### Auto-fill Conditions (Open-Meteo)
When you tap **Auto-fill Conditions**, Peak sends only:
- the session time window, and
- the surf break’s pinned coordinates (latitude/longitude)

to the public Open-Meteo marine + weather APIs over HTTPS, to fill wind, waves, swell, and water temperature.  
This never runs automatically. It does **not** send your name, notes, gear, buddies, photos, or other identifiers.

### Location (optional)
If you choose **Use My Location** or pin a spot on the map, Peak uses location **only** to place that pin (and to enable conditions auto-fill for that pin). Spots can be name-only; location is never required to log a session.

### Apple Health (optional, iPhone)
If you enable Apple Health in Settings:
- Peak can **save** logged surf sessions as surfing workouts.
- Peak can **read** Apple Watch surf workouts, heart rate, and active calories to enrich sessions or offer import.

Health access is system-gated (you can revoke it in iOS Settings). Peak does not upload Health data to any Peak server (there is none).

### Photos & videos
You may attach photos/videos via the system photo picker. Media stays on-device unless you export a full backup or share a session yourself.

### Full backup / export
JSON, CSV, and `.peakbackup` exports are created by you and saved/shared by you. Peak does not transmit them automatically.

## Support contact
If you email support, you may share device details or screenshots in your message. We use that only to respond.

## Changes
If this policy changes, we update this file and the in-app Privacy document.
